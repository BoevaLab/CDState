# Bulkified cancer datasets

To validate CDState performance in datasets with characterized malignant cell states, we generated bulkified cancer datasets using five publicly available single-cell data: 

1. Breast cancer - with seven malignant cell modules characterized by [Wu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-021-00911-1)
2. Ovarian cancer - with eight malignant cell states characterized by [Vázquez-García et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05496-1)
3. Glioblastoma - with eight malignant cell states characterized by [Neftel et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2019.06.024)
4. Lung cancer - with three malignant cell states characterized by [Kim et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-16164-1)
5. Skin squamous cell carcinoma - with four malignant cell states characterized by [Ji et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.05.039).

Bulkified samples were created by pulling all cells of respective cell types/cell states per sample and normalizing the aggregated gene expression by total count. 

Proportions were calculated based on the number of respective cell types/cell states aggregated per sample. 

Ground-truth sources were generated by aggregating cell types of respective cell type/cell state across all samples.
